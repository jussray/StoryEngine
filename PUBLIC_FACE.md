# L99 StoryEngine

L99 StoryEngine is a story-creation operating system that turns a creator's story vision into structured creative work across formats such as books, film, television, podcasts, games, comics, plays, and short-form video.

## What it does

- Starts from a creator's story vision instead of a blank production template.
- Routes the story into a structured run with format and audience context.
- Supports story architecture, chapters, movie beats, IP development, video creation, and other creative workspaces.
- Preserves provenance, workspace boundaries, and promotion/release gates around generated story artifacts.

## Who it is for

StoryEngine is for creators who want one system for developing an idea into structured narrative assets while keeping continuity, provenance, and release decisions explicit.

## Public truth boundary

The repository contains real source and browser workflows, but source behavior is not proof of a current public production deployment. Repository checks, browser tests, provider builds, and live runtime evidence are separate truth planes.

A generated story artifact is not automatically canonical, published, promoted, or released. Those states require their own evidence and authority.
