## Material triggers
Alert only for **shipped official releases**, **credible documented betas**, or **reproducible research** that changes a concrete decision about:
1. Cinematic image-to-video, motion/camera control, multi-shot character/object/scene continuity.
2. Lip-sync, voice timing, speech-driven facial animation and identity persistence.
3. Captions, subtitles, language support, editability and audio synchronization.
4. Remotion/FFmpeg assembly, codecs, alpha, frame/shot metadata, deterministic rendering and exports.
5. Truthful product capture: preserve real screenshots, screen recordings, logos, text and actual product states.

Reject teasers, unrepeatable demos, unsupported claims, routine marketing and duplicate coverage. Distinguish SHIPPED / CREDIBLE_BETA / REPRODUCIBLE_RESEARCH / WATCH / DROP and VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED. Capture provider/model/version, release date, source URL, access limits and material pricing/rights constraints.

## Benchmark contract
For each material alert select **one** smallest controlled comparison, not a broad rebuild:
- Cinematic: fixed still image to a single controlled motion shot.
- Continuity: same subject across two cuts with fixed wardrobe, props and background.
- Sync Avenue: fixed portrait and fixed voice line; measure lip alignment, facial/identity drift and audio timing.
- Truthful capture: composite immutable real product screenshot/recording; compare pixels/text/visible state to source.
- Post-production: run one caption/audio/export pass through the existing Remotion/FFmpeg route.

Record baseline, fixed input, one changed variable, observed output, success criterion, falsifier, source hashes, and available runtime/Playwright evidence. Do not claim product truth from a visually attractive hallucination.
