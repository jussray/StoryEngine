# StoryEngine Repair Authority

This contract replaces the blanket rule that every audit is inspection-only. An audit still does not authorize arbitrary mutation, but when current evidence proves a necessary fix, a trusted operator may repair the plane it owns within the bounded rules below.

StoryEngine's stronger authorship, provenance, consent, privacy, tenant/workspace isolation, canon, and event-history invariants remain load-bearing.

## Necessary-fix default

A trusted acting operator may implement the smallest reversible repair on the existing carrier when the defect is verified and the action stays inside already-established product authority. The operator must preserve unrelated work, bind the action to exact subject fingerprints, retain rollback, and verify after mutation.

Every repair must bind, where applicable, repository/base/head, workspace/tenant/canon subject, event/schema version, provider/runtime identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, before state, rollback, and post-action readback.

## Source and CI authority

Trusted repository operators may:

- repair StoryEngine source, tests, event/schema contracts, cache/memory/canon validators, promotion gates, and documentation;
- add or update the narrowest useful regression test;
- rerun failed GitHub jobs when rerun is the correct diagnostic action;
- repair stale tracker/documentation receipts after authoritative re-observation;
- merge only when the current repository merge policy independently authorizes the exact candidate.

They may not weaken provenance, isolation, consent, revocation, canon locks, auth, promotion gates, or failure signals to obtain green proof.

## Railway/runtime repair authority

A trusted runtime executor may restore StoryEngine's already-established Railway runtime to an exact authorized `main` release when:

- the canonical Railway project/service/environment fingerprint is re-verified;
- the release SHA is exact and already lawful to promote;
- the repair is a normal reversible deployment/configuration operation and does not create new spend or ownership changes;
- secrets are neither exposed nor rotated;
- before-state deployment identity is retained for rollback;
- provider readback verifies the resulting deployment;
- `/runtime-identity`, `/healthz`, and production Playwright are required before the user path is called repaired.

A provider deployment receipt is execution evidence, not proof of user outcome.

## Other witnesses

- GitHub/local tests may trigger source/test/CI repair.
- Playwright/browser may trigger user-path repair and must verify observable fixes.
- Linear/Asana may reopen/update/close tracker records according to stronger evidence but cannot change code/runtime merely from task state.
- Notion/Drive/repository docs may correct stale requirements, decisions, runbooks, and evidence links but cannot rewrite canon/consent/authority silently.
- Slack/internal communication may correct incident/status records but cannot publish creator work, make commercial promises, or create release authority.
- FCR may coordinate cross-project evidence/authority but StoryEngine's tenant/canon/privacy invariants remain local constraints.

## Data and canon hard gates

Explicit founder authority remains required for:

- destructive event, memory, cache, database, artifact, or canon migrations;
- weakening or widening tenant/workspace/auth/page access;
- changing human-locked canon anchors or consent semantics;
- secret creation/exposure/rotation/deletion;
- new billing/spending/contract commitments;
- account/ownership transfer;
- external publication or creator-facing release outside an already-approved publication path;
- authority expansion beyond this contract.

Forward-only, non-destructive repairs that preserve all existing access/privacy/canon boundaries may execute through an already-authorized exact release path when their migration/configuration has been reviewed and rollback/compensation is defined.

## Evidence law

Fingerprints and proof cookies are evidence-only. Tracker state, documentation, model output, provider acceptance, and a prior green packet cannot substitute for current subject-bound proof.

For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
